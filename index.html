Our story

Built from what the tide brought in.

Driftwood Coffee Co started with a single roaster in a garage and a habit of pulling usable timber off the beach after storms. That wood became our counters and shelves — every board a different grain, nothing matching on purpose.

We treat the coffee the same way: sourced from a handful of farms we've visited ourselves, roasted in batches small enough to taste the difference week to week, and served without pretense.
